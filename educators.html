FOR PARENTS, EDUCATORS & CLINICS

The heart lesson most kids never get.

Hearts for Hearts is a free, kid-facing app that explains murmurs, VSDs, and everyday heart health in plain language — built by someone who went through years of checkups without ever really understanding why.

Bring it to your school or clinic See the kids' app

Why kids need this explained, not just diagnosed

1 in 100babies is born with a congenital heart condition — most are as minor as a small VSD
Mostinnocent murmurs and small VSDs resolve on their own with no lasting impact
Rarelyexplained to the child directly, in language they can actually hold onto
  • Plain-language explanations of how the heart works, what a murmur is, and what a VSD is — written for a child, not a chart.
  • A short interactive quiz that reinforces the basics without feeling like homework.
  • Built-in links to find pediatric cardiologists and children's hospitals nearby, for families who need next steps.
  • Every medical claim links back to a credible source — AHA, CDC, Mayo Clinic, KidsHealth, and the British Heart Foundation.

Where this came from

"I was born with a heart murmur and a 2mm VSD — one of the most common heart conditions there is. My checkups went from every 2 months, to 6, to yearly, to every 2 years, and I was cured by 12. But I only ever half-understood what was actually happening. I built Hearts for Hearts because I want kids today to get the heart education I never had."
— Founder, Hearts for Hearts

Bring Hearts for Hearts to your kids

The app itself is free for every family. For schools, pediatric practices, and hospital foundations, there are a few ways to work together.

Classrooms & School Nurses

Use it as a ready-made lesson for health class or a nurse's office resource — no cost, no login required.

Pediatric Clinics
